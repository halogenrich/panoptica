# Quick start

Five minutes to your first batch. The full user guide (the other tab) explains every screen.

## 1. Sign in

- Open the address the lab gave you (on a phone: scan the QR code from someone's profile menu, **Invite a phone**).
- Sign in with the username and temporary password from the admin. The first time, the app asks you to choose your own password.
- Your initial (top right) opens your profile: theme, default visibility (private or shared) and password.

## 2. Find or make the recipes

- Recipes are in the sidebar (on a phone: the **Recipes** tab): substrate prep, solutions, spin coating, organic and metal evaporation.
- Look at **Shared** first: the lab's standard recipes are already there. Open one to see the full procedure.
- **New …** makes your own. Changing a recipe that someone uses saves a new version (*Name v2*), so nothing used in a batch changes behind anyone's back.

## 3. Build a stack

- **Stacks › New stack**: choose p-i-n or n-i-p, then pick a recipe for each layer. The 3D view grows as you go.
- Or open a shared stack and use it as it is.

## 4. Start a batch and work at the bench

- In the stack, type a batch code (or leave it empty for an automatic one) and press **Start batch**.
- **Print sheet** gives a blank sheet for the bench, or use the batch on your phone.
- Tick each layer when it is done. **Add run data** next to a recipe records what really happened (masses, volumes, rates, thicknesses, deviations).

## 5. Import the J–V measurements

- **Electric measurements › Import measurements**: drop the folder the J–V system saved (or a .zip of it).
- Link each sample to your batch, and under **Devices for the plots** untick bad devices or give device groups (for example "control" and "+ PEAI") to compare.
- Click a pixel for its J–V curve and **Export PNG**; **Statistics** has the box plots and **Export CSV**.

## 6. Share and keep

- Comments: every stack, recipe and batch has comments (with replies and reactions). The owner is notified (the bell, top right).
- **Report (PDF)** and **Spreadsheet** on a batch page give everything about the batch in one file, for your notebook, a thesis or a paper.
- Deleted something by mistake? **Undo** right after deleting, or **Trash** in the sidebar (kept for 30 days).
